How scatter-pay works

8–9

Any 8 or 9 of the same symbol anywhere → pays the first tier multiplier.

10–11

Ten or eleven → second-tier multiplier. Winning cells are removed; fresh symbols drop in.

12+

Twelve or more → top-tier multiplier. Multiple symbols can win simultaneously from one grid.

Cascade

After each win, removed cells collapse, new symbols fall in, and the grid is scored again — up to 8 times per spin.
